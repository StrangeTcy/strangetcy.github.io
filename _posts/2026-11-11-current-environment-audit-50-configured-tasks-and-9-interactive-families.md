**There are confirmed defects that change what the scores mean.** They include supplied diagnoses in every original category environment, broken visible-test imports in all six unusual-substrate environments, prompt/judge disagreements that reject reasonable solutions, and judges that accept a weaker mathematical property than the task requests. The nine newer recursive category environments and nine interactive epistemic families show cleaner inspected public/private boundaries; that scoped finding is not a certification of their complete implementations.

Audited on 11 November 2026. Repository: [StrangeTcy/rl_eval_generator](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/rl_eval_generator/tree/42295b3aece5d24109a20d08e4a39a19bdba47e9), HEAD `42295b3aece5d24109a20d08e4a39a19bdba47e9`. The [inventory](https://strangetcy.github.io/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/current-environment-configs.txt) contains 50 `envs/**/config.yaml` files. The nine `arena/epistemic_families` implementations are counted separately. Source remained unchanged during the audit. Some inspected files were not present in that commit; their citations link to published source snapshots verified against the audit's recorded SHA-256 hashes. This was a source and archived-evidence audit; no Python process, paid model call, or fresh campaign was run.

“Confirmed” below means the source establishes the defect, mismatch, or exposed cue, sometimes with an archived result showing its effect. “Candidate” requires a targeted reproduction before changing a result. “Scoped” means no glaring defect of the inspected kind was found; it does not mean every branch or oracle was verified. A deliberate hint or weak baseline can be appropriate in a guided exercise. It becomes a measurement problem when the result is interpreted as independent diagnosis, domain recognition, or a stronger theorem than the judge checks.

## 1. Prompt/judge disagreements with observed consequences

**GNN aggregation.** The [prompt](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/semiring/gnn_message_passing/files/prompt.md#L5) asks for permutation equivariance, while the [judge](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/semiring/gnn_message_passing/files/judge.py#L34) requires exactly `A h`. A residual `h + A h` is equivariant too. In the reviewed archive, all 18 candidate permutation tests pass; some submissions fail exact equality hidden in `basic_run`. State the exact aggregation operator, or accept the allowed class of equivariant operators and enforce nontriviality separately.

**Physical allocation.** The [routing prompt](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/sheaf/sheaf_physical_constraints/files/prompt.md#L1) requests safe routing under shared capacities. The judge additionally requires a [5:3 ratio](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/sheaf/sheaf_physical_constraints/files/judge.py#L66), [utilization](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/sheaf/sheaf_physical_constraints/files/judge.py#L94), and symmetry. A reviewed Atria max-min allocation is feasible but fails the proportional-allocation convention. State the fairness objective and clipping/scaling order; score feasibility separately. Other reviewed algorithms also have real order/symmetry defects, so this finding does not excuse every nonpass.

**BatchNorm file shape.** The [prompt explicitly promises](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/batchnorm_ema/files/prompt.md#L20) that the grader scores behavior rather than a particular patch shape. The [judge nevertheless requires](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/batchnorm_ema/files/judge.py#L104) edits to both model and trainer. Mercury's all-easy patch configures BN momentum in the trainer, reaches accuracy 1.0, and passes the BN behavior diagnostics, yet receives FAIL/0.95 solely for missing a `model.py` edit. Both the promise and outcome are preserved in the archive. Another Mercury case reaches 0.984 with the same file-only failure. Judge the normalization behavior, or disclose and score a separate workflow constraint. Glyph and MoCo also require multiple file edits, but the sampled Glyph result at 0.819 also misses its 0.85 accuracy threshold and is not a file-only false negative.

**One-step payload representation.** The [prompt](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/trajectory_semantics/one_step/files/prompt.md#L8) requests the labels in the supplied specification, with integer payloads for canonical cases. The [judge maps](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/trajectory_semantics/one_step/files/judge.py#L26) red/blue to 0/1 and [supplies integers](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/trajectory_semantics/one_step/files/judge.py#L74) even in renamed cases. Reasoning Atria's valid/reflective solver passes six of seven checks; its sole failure returns the literal blue for `Kestrel(blue)`. That follows a natural reading of the prompt. Document one input/output encoding and implement it consistently.

**Regex substrate.** The [prompt forbids character loops](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/weird_machine/regex_state_machine/files/prompt.md#L25). The [judge additionally rejects](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/weird_machine/regex_state_machine/files/judge.py#L47) direct indexing on a function argument but accepts analogous host-language indexing via a padded alias. Reviewed Mercury direct-index variants receive 0.75 while an alias-based variant passes. Decide whether callbacks with host indexing are allowed, state the policy, and apply it consistently to equivalent programs. A name-sensitive AST rule does not establish computation in regex.

**Safe parsing in the monad task.** [The source names unsafe evaluation](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/monadic_reward/files/agent_code.py#L8), but [the import policy](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/monadic_reward/config.yaml#L32) does not allow `ast` and the prompt supplies no sanctioned alternative. Eight Mercury and nine reasoning-Atria submissions are rejected specifically for importing `ast`; another Mercury case fails action parsing. Ordinary Atria has a passing manual parser, so the task is not proved impossible. Permit safe literal parsing, supply a parser, or explicitly define the restricted grammar and allowed API. These rejections do not measure state-monad understanding.

## 2. Tasks that disclose the answer or demand unavailable information

**Every original category task exposes its diagnosis.** All 17 old templates place an answer-bearing comment in the agent workspace. The matrix gives the exact file and line for each. Examples include the untouched-coordinate setter bug, the soft-min formula, missing capacity constraints, the tokenizer space-marker replacement, and eager versus lazy sampling. The comments persist in hard variants. Removing the word `BUG` while preserving the explanation or formula would leave most of the leakage intact.

**Recurrent, MoCo, and RoPE leaks persist across clue settings.** The [state-carry source](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/recurrent_depth/state_carry/files/recurrent_block.py#L18), [gradient-credit source](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/recurrent_depth/gradient_credit/files/recurrent_block.py#L16), and [halting source](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/recurrent_depth/adaptive_halting/files/halting.py#L18) diagnose the exact repair even when other hints are empty. MoCo's [queue docstring](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/moco/files/queue_ops.py#L10) names the wraparound failure. RoPE's [attention](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/rope/files/attention.py#L30), [cache](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/rope/files/cache.py#L8), and [rotation](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/rope/files/rope.py#L18) sources identify missing offset/state updates. Its [visible test](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/rope/files/visible_tests.py#L7) contains a complete correct rotation implementation; the local train/eval helpers contain it too. Deriving the construction from the paper is unnecessary under that interface.

**Distractors advertise that they are distractors.** Glyph [labels an augmentation bug irrelevant](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/glyph/config.yaml#L99), calls a [hard test misleading](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/glyph/config.yaml#L135), and labels a [proxy intervention](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/glyph/config.yaml#L263). MoCo's [hard test](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/moco/config.yaml#L138) also announces that loss can decrease under collapse. These statements resolve part of the intended evidence-selection task. Easy hints intentionally controlled by an axis should be retained as labeled assistance conditions; fixed hints in supposedly harder conditions should be removed.

**Partial deception asks for information it withholds.** In [the generator](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/rl_eval_generator/blob/42295b3aece5d24109a20d08e4a39a19bdba47e9/shared/deception_game.py#L193), accurate and falsehood branches can produce the same positive statement and the same access evidence while the independent record's outcome is redacted. The required exact mechanism labels differ. Source inspection establishes equality of substantive public evidence, not necessarily byte-identical ordering: the shuffle seed itself includes mechanism, creating a potential answer-correlated ordering artifact. Supply identifying evidence or accept an uncertainty/set-valued answer for that equivalence class; randomize order independently of the label.

The same generator labels “could inspect” as [knowing](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/rl_eval_generator/blob/42295b3aece5d24109a20d08e4a39a19bdba47e9/shared/deception_game.py#L194) and assigns [counterfactual audit behavior](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/rl_eval_generator/blob/42295b3aece5d24109a20d08e4a39a19bdba47e9/shared/deception_game.py#L224) by a lookup rule not justified by a public behavioral model. Opportunity, observation, and knowledge need separate representations. An exact counterfactual target needs an explicit policy, utility model, or identifying observations.

**Signaling prints the target policy.** The [public task](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/rl_eval_generator/blob/42295b3aece5d24109a20d08e4a39a19bdba47e9/shared/deception_game.py#L119) gives the complete 2×2 signal/receiver-action table plus the private type and incentive used to [choose the target](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/rl_eval_generator/blob/42295b3aece5d24109a20d08e4a39a19bdba47e9/shared/deception_game.py#L233). This is a policy-reading control, not a test of discovering a signaling equilibrium. Either label it accordingly or supply utilities and information from which a strategy must be derived.

## 4. Broken setup, ineffective controls, and reproduction risks

All six unusual-substrate `visible_tests.py` files import `%%MODEL_FILE%%`, whose values include `.py`. The result is an import such as `from ci_machine.py import CIDependencyGraph`, which asks for a submodule of a flat module. It is a wrong module path, not intrinsically a syntax error. The matrix links each source line. Archived Mercury traces show attempts to repair these tests. Separate module names from filenames and smoke-test the generated visible tests before a model sees them.

Nine category axis substitutions are unconsumed by local templates and have no special renderer handling: CHECK_DIM, STRICT_LAWS, LR_VAL, SYMMETRY_DIM, AUG_PARAM, SIDE_EFFECT_CHECK, TOLERANCE, SIGMA_VAL, and UNICODE_SUPPORT. Their environments appear in the matrix, and [the category audit](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/category-current-audit.md) supplies each config line. Optimizer MODEL_CLASS naming is also unused. A metadata or derived-seed change does not make these into the advertised semantic manipulation. RoPE's [HIDDEN_OFFSET declaration](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/rope/files/judge.py#L21) is similarly unused: the probe hard-codes offset 5 although [config levels](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/rope/config.yaml#L98) specify 32/257/1027.

The augmentation judge restricts outputs to C4 rotations and requires at least three nonidentity samples in ten draws. The public prompt mentions 90-degree rotational invariance as an example but does not explicitly impose C4-only outputs, prohibit reflections, or disclose that quota. Rotation invariance does not imply reflection invariance; nevertheless the accepted group needs to be part of the contract before every D4 mismatch is called a model error. The unseeded quota also rejects a correct uniform C4 sampler with probability 436/1,048,576, about 0.0416%. This small lottery does not explain the reviewed Atria failures, whose patches add reflections outside the judge's accepted orbit. Specify the group and nontriviality requirement, then use controlled coverage.

Two candidates need isolated preflight reproduction:

- **Schema baseline sufficiency.** [Medium/hard schemas](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/sheaf/sheaf_schema_sync/config.yaml#L21) already defer foreign keys, and the [starter](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/cat_theo/sheaf/sheaf_schema_sync/files/migration.py#L43) commits after all insertions. Ordinary SQLite semantics suggest the nominally broken baseline already meets the target. Verify before calling those cells repair tasks.
- **Exact floating-point halting equality.** The [judge](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/recurrent_depth/adaptive_halting/files/judge.py#L51) compares against a full-batch reference with exact equality. Mercury's mathematically equivalent active-subset/cloned-state implementation scores 0.8 with only per-example equality failing. Different matrix-operation shapes can change rounding. Measure maximum error and retest with a justified tolerance. The five other Mercury leaf-tensor mutation failures are separate, confirmed execution errors.

Dependency reproduction also needs attention. The [agent image](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/shared/Dockerfile.agent#L4) installs unpinned torch/torchvision; the [judge image](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/shared/Dockerfile.judge#L4) pins 2.5.1/0.20.1. This can produce different local and grading behavior and makes rebuilds time-dependent. It does not prove that the archived images differed. Pin compatible versions and record resolved image/package digests for both sides.

## 5. Trust boundaries: what the audit did and did not establish

The agent image [copies workspace and tools](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/shared/Dockerfile.agent#L11); the judge image [separately copies judge code](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/shared/Dockerfile.judge#L11). The older epistemic layouts keep their full instance and semantics on the judge side. No general exposure of judge-private files in the agent workspace was established. The RoPE reference finding concerns files deliberately placed on the public side.

The [AST validator explicitly disclaims being a security sandbox](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/shared/source_validator.py#L1). Some judges [import the candidate in their own interpreter](https://github.com/StrangeTcy/strangetcy.github.io/blob/main/assets/research/capability-audit-2026-11-11/source-snapshots/envs/recurrent_depth/adaptive_halting/files/judge.py#L39). Container isolation protects the host boundary; it does not by itself make shared-process scoring tamper-proof. Treat in-process scoring integrity as a candidate risk requiring a controlled negative test, not a demonstrated exploit. The newer recursive judges generally evaluate subprocess-returned certificates against private logic, which is a useful separation to preserve.

## 8. A practical repair and validation sequence

1. Correct the declared contracts and impossible/underspecified targets first. Record which old verdicts are affected; preserve the original campaign record and publish a separate reinterpretation.
2. Repair setup failures and fix the agent/judge dependency versions. Generate every configured level and confirm its visible tests import and run as intended.
3. Split guided and uncued conditions. Search every agent-readable artifact for diagnoses, expected formulas, complete reference code, and labels that disclose experimental roles. Keep specifications, APIs, and legitimate task data.
4. Make each intended semantic factor reach an executed behavior. Use generated-artifact comparisons plus targeted probes; remove inert factors or label them as controls.
5. For each generated instance, run the original baseline, a known-correct reference, and a known-wrong negative control. Add the specific counterexamples above. Treat reference timeout as judge failure rather than mathematical unsatisfiability.
6. Re-run selected paired model tasks only after those controls pass. Preserve behavior scores, import/action/patch errors, workflow requirements, and explanation review as distinct outcomes.
